**Historical report.** The current verification level and corrections are described in [pass 1.2.0](COMPILER_PASS_120.md). The old aggregate figures are not a new independent run. Specific source files from the portable selection were rerun together with their dependencies; discrepancies found are preserved.

20 November 2026, **Delphi 13 Community Edition (`IDE 37.0.60542.8024`)**.
All builds were performed through bridge `MCP2Delphi` using the standard `IDE Build` action in isolated directories (`Win32` `0x14c` and `Win64` `0x8664`, `Debug` configurations and `Release` controls), after warming up the IDE Messages window (`P11_Warmup_Messages`), so every probe accepted into the portable package contains a genuine `Building <Id>.dproj (...)` line and `compile_events`.

In addition to the `208` builds in series `P1–P10` ([pass 0.11](COMPILER_PASS_011.md)), the work toward release **`1.0.0`** performed another **`151` builds (`P11–P14`)** — a total of **`359` working IDE Builds (`P1–P14`)**:
- **Series `P11` (`26` runs):** bitwise verification of all PE32/PE32+ flags (`ALLOWBIND`, `ALLOWISOLATION`, `NXCOMPAT`, `DYNAMICBASE`, `HIGHENTROPYVA`, `TSAWARE`, `LARGEADDRESSAWARE`, `SETPEFLAGS`/`SETPEOPTFLAGS`, `SETPEUSERVERSION`, `MINSTACKSIZE`/`MAXSTACKSIZE`), writing `DESCRIPTION` twice (`.rdata` ASCII + `.rsrc` UTF-16LE), `P11_Extension32.bin`, `my_P11_LibNaming_Dll32_v1.2.0.dll`, live linking of a synthetic OMF `.obj` (`P11_Link_Obj32_OMF2` → `omf2=42` with `PUBDEF 'ObjAdd'` without leading `_` versus `E2065`), `P11_DenyPackageUnit32`, and explicit recording of an internal IDE AV (`delphicoreide370.bpl`) when building a `.dpk` through `MCP2Delphi`.
- **Series `P12` (`39` runs):** all remaining language directives (`TSAWARE OFF` → `DllChars=0x140`, `CODEALIGN 16`, `OLDTYPELAYOUT`, `TYPEDADDRESS` `E2010` vs `byte0=65`, `IOCHECKS`, `OVERFLOWCHECKS`, `EXCESSPRECISION`, `REALCOMPATIBILITY` `6` vs `8` B, `SAFEDIVIDE`, `OPENSTRINGS` `10` vs `255`, `VARSTRINGCHECKS`, `HINTS`, `WARN` `OFF`/`ERROR` `E1000`, `MESSAGE` `H1054`/`W1054`/`E1054`, `STACKFRAMES`, `METHODINFO`, `WEAKLINKRTTI`/`STRONGLINKTYPES`, `IMPORTEDDATA`, `VARPROPSETTER` with mutation of the caller's variable `caller_x=110` versus `E2282`); an 8-step bytewise `DbgProbeU.dcu` size ladder (`1110` → `1155` → `1187` [`$D0`=`D-` with active `$L+`/`$YD`] → `1189` → bytewise-equal `1262 B` for `$D1`/`$D2`/`$YD` → `1295 B` `$Y+`); `.hpp`/`.obj` generation under `-JPHNE` (`EXTERNALSYM`, `NODEFINE`, `OBJTYPENAME`, `OBJEXPORTALL`), and a 6-variant `{$NOINCLUDE}` matrix (a case-insensitive Pascal identifier from `uses` versus `E2003` for `.hpp`/`.pas` and a truncated scoped name).
- **Series `P13` (`84` runs: `82` main + `2` precision micro-follow-ups for `{$EXTENDEDCOMPATIBILITY ON}`):**
  1. `IF_UNDECLARED_IDENTIFIER_OBSERVATION` and `IF_TYPED_CONSTANT_OBSERVATION`: all `12/12` comparisons of an unknown name and all `24/24` comparisons of a typed constant (under `J-` and `J+`, `Win32` and `Win64`) simultaneously produced `True`, whereas `not Unknown` → `False` and `not TypedConst` → `E2012` ([detailed matrix](IF_UNDECLARED_PROBES.md)).
  2. `GENERIC_ARGUMENT_DOC_COUNTEREXAMPLE`: a 12-type `TBox<T>` matrix — `10` types work with `Default(T)` and `SizeOf(T)`, anonymous `TBox<string[5]>` is rejected (`E2003`/`E2008`), and `file of Integer` (`592 B`) and `TextFile` (`730 B`) work without `Default(T)`, but with `Value := Default(T)` cause internal failure **`F2084 Internal Error: NC2130`**.
  3. `INLINE_DEFAULT_PROFILE`: `IFOPT INLINE = ON` in `Debug` and `Release`; physical counting of constant `$13572468` in sections `.text`/`.itext` proved 3 inlined copies in `.itext` with both sides `ON` (even in `Debug`), suppression of inlining with `{$INLINE OFF}` on either side, and absence of automatic cross-unit inlining without directive `inline;` under `{$INLINE AUTO}`.
  4. `EXTENDED_TARGET_PROFILE`: `P13_ExtCompat_Prec_On64` vs `On32` proved that under `{$EXTENDEDCOMPATIBILITY ON}` on `Win64`, size and offsets match `Win32` (`SizeOf=10`, `Unpacked=24/offE=8/offB=18`, `Packed=12/offE=1/offB=11`), but `1.0 + 1e-17 <> 1.0` produces **`prec64=FALSE`** (a 53-bit `Double` mantissa), and a round-trip through `TExtended80Rec` produces **`rt_eq=FALSE`** (versus `TRUE/TRUE` on `Win32`).
  5. `NAMEOF_DETAILED_GRAMMAR`: another 12 forms tested (`NameOf(TBox<Integer>) → 'TBox'`, `NameOf(System.Classes.TComponent) → 'TComponent'`, `Result`, `Over`, `SMyRes`, `enAlpha`; rejection with `E2233` for `TMyClass.Prop`, `E2372` for `Prop`/`C.Prop`/`inherited`, `E2029` for a unit name).
  6. `INCLUDE_STATEMENT_PART`: all 5 additional positions (`InExpr`, `InRecord`, `InCase`, `BetweenVarBegin`, `InUses`) accepted with `exit = 0` and `0` diagnostics.
  7. `EXTRA_DIRECTIVES_NOT_REVIEWED`: `{$OLDOBJWARN ON}` on `object`/`virtual object` produced `0` warnings; modifier `unsafe;` was rejected with `E1030` even with `{$UNSAFECODE ON}` (`W1002`); the other 5 were accepted with `0` diagnostics ([EXTRA_OPTIONS.md](EXTRA_OPTIONS.md)).
  8. `IFOPT_TABLE_TYPOS`: `14` new long forms confirmed (a total of `17` with `P3`/`P8`, including `SETPEOSVERSION=OFF`, `TSAWARE=ON`, `INLINE=ON`), while table misspelling `SETOSVERSION` was rejected with `E1030`.
  9. `LONGSTRINGS_DOC_CONFLICT`: project option `.dproj` `<DCC_LongStrings>false</DCC_LongStrings>` passed `-$H-` (`IFOPT_H=MINUS`), but in all 4 positions (`local`, `global`, `field`, `param`) on `Win32` and `Win64`, `string` remained `UnicodeString`.
  10. `PROGRAM_CONTROL_TARGET_COPY` and `WIN64_ASSEMBLER_DOC_DETAILS`: the precise `const P: TRecN` passing grid (`1..24 B`) revealed the **`3 B`** asymmetry (by value on `Win32`, by reference on `Win64`; `8 B` `const` on `Win64` is also by reference!), while `P13_Win64_Xmm45_Preserve` directly showed that an ordinary Delphi call overwrites `XMM4` and `XMM5` (`pres4=FALSE`, `pres5=FALSE`) and preserves `XMM6` (`pres6=TRUE`).
- **Series `P14` (`2` runs):** the historical report recorded execution of `P14_BookCh47_1` and `P14_BookCh52_1`. The earlier coverage claim of `81/81` (`100%`) was withdrawn in 1.2.0: the original list included a template with an ellipsis.
